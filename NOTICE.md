## Open Filament Database

With the **Open Filament Database** option on (Settings > Connection, off by default), U1 Control reads preset data from the [Open Filament Database](https://openfilamentdatabase.org) at run time. None of it ships with U1 Control. That data is Copyright © 2025 OpenFilamentCollective, under the MIT licence: <https://github.com/OpenFilamentCollective/open-filament-database/blob/main/LICENSE>.
